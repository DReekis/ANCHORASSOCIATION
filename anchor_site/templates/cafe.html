The Signature

The Earthen Cup

Every drink at Bagan Bilash is served in a handmade kullad — a clay cup shaped by local potters. It keeps your chai warm, adds an earthy flavor, and supports artisan livelihoods.

No plastic. No paper. Just earth, fire, and flavor.
