Life at Anchor

Guided by Purpose.

Hear from the voices steering the Anchor Association.

"True empowerment begins when we bridge the gap between academic excellence and social responsibility. ​At the heart of every thriving community lies a strong anchor—a foundation of stability, growth, and collective responsibility. Our organization was founded on a simple yet profound belief: that progress is multidimensional. Through our social welfare programs, academic support centers, and community development initiatives, we are committed to uplifting every facet of society. ​Whether we are shaping young minds at Anchor Public School, providing critical resources through the Anchor Association, or fostering grassroots development, our goal remains the same: to empower the individual and strengthen the collective. Our work is not limited to a single field. We are building an integrated ecosystem where quality education, academic coaching, and social welfare programs work in harmony. We invite you to join us in this journey of service, excellence, and sustainable change as we work together to secure a brighter future for all. "

Mousumi Dey

President

B.Sc.(Hons), LLB (Hons), MSW

"At the heart of ANCHOR Association lies a commitment to holistic community development. As Treasurer and Project Head, my role transcends mere numbers; it is about ensuring that every resource we mobilize is transformed into a tangible impact. From the foundational education at Anchor Public School and Minions Fun House to the economic empowerment driven by Nivedita SHG and Career Edge, our projects are designed to create a self-sustaining ecosystem of growth. Transparency is the cornerstone of our financial philosophy. Like any mission-driven organization, we face the ongoing challenge of rising operational costs and the need for more robust infrastructure to meet increasing demand. Scaling our charitable initiatives, such as CURE Homes and Learn and Shine, requires consistent capital and strategic fund-raising. We are currently navigating these hurdles by optimizing our internal expenses and diversifying our donor base to ensure that no project remains underfunded. Despite the challenges, our trajectory remains upward. We have seen remarkable success with our core projects: Education: Anchor Public School and Minions Fun House continue to set benchmarks in primary and kindergarten education. Empowerment: The Nivedita SHG has successfully integrated more women into the local economy. Employment: Career Edge is bridging the gap between talent and opportunity, providing vital job consultancy services. This steady progress is a testament to our disciplined financial management and the unwavering dedication of our project teams. The future of the ANCHOR Association is one of expansion and integration. We are looking toward digitizing our learning programs like ATMA Katha and expanding the reach of our charitable healthcare services. Our goal is to move from localized impact to a broader regional presence, ensuring that "Anchor" remains synonymous with stability, empowerment, and progress. We invite you to join us on this journey—whether as a partner, a donor, or a volunteer—as we build a future where every individual has the tools to shine."

Atanu Konar

Treasurer & Project Head

B.com (Hons.), PGD (HRM), MBA (Marketing)

True education, we believe, prepares a child to live with awareness, compassion, and purpose."
